      How Can I Invest in Silver in Kerala?

      The following are the different forms of silver available in Kerala where you can invest in: 

      • Silver commodity - Trading in silver is a profitable and preferred investment option and investors can either invest in silver futures or spot silver. Investing in silver ETFs through Bombay Stock Exchange is also a preferred choice among most investors in Kerala, though one must be aware of the rules and regulations before indulging in it. 
      • Silver coins and bars - In Kerala, an individual can invest in silver coins and bars through various private and public sector banks. Investors can purchase internationally certified silver from banks which cost slightly more yet are of high quality compared to any retail store. 
      • Silver contracts - Individuals can trade in silver futures through MCX or NCDEX as this is quite a popular investment option in Kerala. It is easy to trade in silver contracts as it offers internationally competitive rates, minimum transaction fees, and a simple and transparent pricing mechanism. 
      • Physical silver - Investing in physical silver is the most traditional and common form of investment. Individuals can invest in silver jewelry and silver wares which offers high re-selling value and offers good returns over other investment options.

      What are the Major Factors that Influence the Silver Rates in Kerala?

      Silver prices in India fluctuates depending on several factors. Here are some of the major factors that impact the price of silver in Kerala: 

      • The dollar's value impacts the international silver rates, which affects the cost of silver in the country. 
      • Increasing industrial demand for silver with respect to the rate of supply influences the silver price in Kerala. 
      • The oil prices daily also impact the international market, which affects the silver price in Kerala. 
      • Silver is imported to meet the increasing demand for the metal, which in turn imposes import duty on the silver rate, thereby influencing the cost of the white metal. 

      What is BIS Hallmarked Silver Rate in Kerala?

      BIS Hallmark scheme was introduced by Bureau of Indian Standards for silver in 2005. Here are the following authentications that a hallmark silver consists of: 

      • Unique code letter mentioning the year of manufacture 
      • Government of India assigned number or identification hallmark 
      • BIS hallmark to authenticate the purity and genuinity of the silver 
      • Grades of 970, 925, 900, 835 or 800 for alloys of silver, silver jewelry, and silver artefacts to indicate the purity of the silver item 
      • Identification mark of the jeweler

      FAQs on Silver Rate in Kerala

      1.What factors affect the silver rate in Kerala?

      Some factors which affect the silver rate in Kerala are the trend of the bullion market overseas, the trend of the equities market. The value of the dollar and the rupee in the market also plays a role in the changing silver prices.

      2.What purchasing alternatives are available for silver?

      Coins, jewelry, bars, cutlery, and ornaments can all be acquired in the form of the priceless white metal.

      3.What are the factors that I should consider while buying silver?

      There are several factors that should be considered before purchasing silver products in Kerala. Check the current silver rate in the city, hallmarking and certification, and the weight of the silver item, before making an investment. 

      4.Is it possible to buy silver coins in Kerala?

      Yes, silver coins can be bought in Kerala.

      5.How do I buy silver at the best rates in Kerala?

      To buy silver at the best rates in Kerala, perform a market analysis and get the available prices offered by different jewelers. Consider the best rates and ensure the topmost silver quality along with the most suitable buy-back policy offered by the jeweler. 

      6.Where can I buy the best quality silver in Kerala?

      You can buy the best quality silver from the leading silver retailers and jewellers in Kerala.

      7.Where do I get the best quality silver products in Kerala?

      In Kerala, you can find various trusted silver retailers and established silver jewelry stores where you can get the best quality of silver products. Ensure to check the necessary certifications to authenticate the silver purity and the ongoing silver rates. 

      8.Can silver bars be purchased at jewellery shops in Kerala?

      Yes, silver bars can be purchased at jewelry shops.

      9.When is the best time to purchase silver in Kerala?

      The expensive white metal is best purchased when it is on sale. But you should routinely keep an eye on silver prices in order to be aware of pricing changes.

      10.Can silver bars be purchased at private sector banks in Kerala?

      Yes, silver bars can be purchased at private sector banks.

      11.Does the silver price in Kerala change daily?

      The silver rate in Kerala may change frequently due to many factors including the trend of the bullion and equities market. The price of crude oil also plays an important role in the change in silver rate along with the value of the rupee and the dollar in the international market.

      12.Will GST have to be paid if I buy silver?

      Yes, GST will have to be paid if you buy silver.

      13.How is the silver rate derived?

      The daily silver rate is determined by considering the dollar value, change in policy, import duties, geopolitical factors, price of crude oil industrial demand, and other considerable factors.

      14.Is there a best time when silver can be purchased in Kerala?

      Most investors usually purchase silver when the economy and financial markets are down. Silver rates fall during this period.

      15.Should I buy silver coins or silver bars in Kerala?

      Purchasing silver coins is typically a great investment choice if you only have a small sum of money available. However, if you want to invest a sizable sum of money, silver bars are your best bet. This strategy can help you produce effective results.

      16.Can silver bars be purchased at public sector banks in Kerala?

      Yes, silver bars can be purchased at public sector banks.
